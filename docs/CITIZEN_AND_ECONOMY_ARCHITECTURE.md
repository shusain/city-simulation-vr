## City loans (D90, schema65)

FCityLoanState in the authoritative economy snapshot owns one external civic-lender contract. Fixed offers are5k/10k/20k over24 monthly equal-principal installments, 0.5% interest on outstanding principal rounded up to integer dollars. Principal due is the outstanding balance less the remaining scheduled principal (original principal times months remaining /24, integer floor). Month-end decreases the remaining term, charges interest, and pays available treasury cash to interest first and due principal second, after existing operating/public-payroll settlement. Unpaid amounts remain owed after maturity; interest arrears do not compound. Full early payoff debits outstanding principal plus accrued interest and clears the contract, with no future interest or fee.

Borrowing transfers external lender -> city; repayment transfers city -> lender. Neither is tax income or operating upkeep. Pending borrowed/principal-paid/interest-paid command flows persist and join the next settled monthly snapshot, so save/load between command and settlement cannot erase or duplicate a transfer. Last settled flows and current balances are separate. Debt service belongs in budget expenses/Net; LastUpkeep remains the existing operating bill for payroll/service-shortfall logic. The profitable-month objective deducts loan repayments and requires zero arrears. Reports append principal/unpaid-dues stocks and borrowed/principal-paid/interest-paid/interest-charged flows; annual and quarter debt coverage stays unknown when any observed month predates recording.

Loan UI previews are read-only shared Loans/LoanReview pages with native commit guards. Current debt is visible immediately; newspaper/finance histories describe closing monthly facts. Schema65 validates offer principal, nonnegative bounded flows, issue/settlement dates and exact remaining term; older-format documents remain available for archival rather than receiving invented debt/history. Terms are provisional balance defaults. Refinancing, multiple loans, penalties/foreclosure, policies and post-AI economics are separate future slices; physical headset acceptance is unconfirmed.

## Household migration rules (D89, schema62 foundation)

After monthly settlement and happiness assessment, an existing home's residents search together when their mean actual happiness and outlook are below50 and at least half have three consecutive low months. Homeless residents search individually. A destination must be fully vacant, operational and large enough for the whole group, with a home assessment of at least50 and at least10 points above the source outlook. Stable home IDs break ties. The assessment uses retained completed purchases and savings after the moving fee; it does not invent new purchases or an education allocation. Provisional fees equal one household goods-order budget (minimum one order/unit price). Stable citizen-ID order debits wallets to external movers; treasury and shop stock do not change.

A monthly administrative transfer retains citizen identity, education, job and household purchase history. Incompatible purchase locks are released without a sale/refund, and stale trips/lane occupancy are removed through existing traffic reconciliation. Citizens resume their home routine at the destination. Actual happiness and low-month history remain; the improved outlook changes future assessments. No moving-van animation or per-citizen Actor is introduced. A three-month settling cooldown limits repeated moves. Active care, custody and pending/detained arson associations delay the group, preventing orphaned records.

If at least half reach six unresolved low months with no viable affordable destination, the group leaves. Departure removes its citizen/household records and records exactly the remaining wallet money carried out. Improved outlook prevents departure while mood recovers. Vacated homes are held for three months; departures also pause new admissions citywide for three months so automatic capacity filling cannot hide population loss. Searching households reserve their own unused capacity and a vacant candidate from newcomers. Normal admission resumes after the finite cooldown using new monotonic identities. Physical homes survive unemployment and temporary outages; available operational housing can rehouse homeless citizens without a job prerequisite.

Schema62 persists migration clocks, statuses, recent moves, vacancy holds and four cumulative ledgers: residents relocated, residents departed, moving fees paid outside the city and money carried out. Current-version malformed/future/conflicting states reject transactionally. Earlier-schema cities preserve applicable happiness but start fresh migration state; prior report observations are not backfilled. PEOPLE and FINANCE reports expose observed cumulative totals, with annual closing values rather than summing them repeatedly. Closing resident/employment/consumption census is refreshed after migration without rebilling completed transactions. Quarterly sentiment news, policy administration, loans, richer purpose/preferences and post-AI institutions remain D90 work.

## Citizen happiness foundation (D89, schema61)

Citizen records now carry Happiness, HappinessTarget, HappinessConcerns, DiscontentMonths and LastHappinessMonth. Scores are0–100. New arrivals and earlier-schema loads start at60 with no invented discontent history; their assessment clock starts at the current billed month. The live subsystem assesses once at the closing monthly phase, after settlement, education and derived services. Display, forecasts, reconciliation, pause and load never assess. Each actual assessment moves happiness at most10 points toward the target. Below50 is discontent; three consecutive observed months below50 is sustained. Reaching50 clears the streak. A direct skipped-month call supplies one observation and restarts the streak rather than fabricating missing observations. Live fine/coarse catch-up still processes each completed month.

The initial provisional formula starts at85. An operating park adds its bounded appeal/3 (maximum10); an actual allocated education place adds5. A home operation failure subtracts25; otherwise unpaid city operating bills subtract10. Unmet completed household purchases subtract up to30, proportional to current household orders; reserved/projected purchases do not satisfy this. A household with less than one goods-order budget in remaining shared savings loses10. Pollution subtracts up to20 using the existing pollution desirability penalty; crime risk subtracts up to15 using the existing neighborhood risk penalty. Waste uses the existing post-grace pressure capped10. Residential tax above the default10% subtracts one point per additional percentage point, capped15. Illness subtracts10 mild/20 severe; actual remote/walk-in/hospital treatment reduces that penalty by5. The result clamps0–100. Concern flags describe nonzero penalties, not inferred motives or a separate opinion simulation.

A citizen without housing loses35 for housing and30 for the current lack of home-based shopping access, plus10 with no personal reserves. Custody does not invent those homelessness penalties when no physical home exists. Unemployment and educational attainment alone never penalize happiness: household resources, actual consumption/access and available learning matter. Household purchase demand is the current allocation and completed purchases are the latest settled visits; assessments do not reconstruct former household membership/demand. Current moving-house rules are above.

Shared city overview shows resident-weighted average happiness and the number below50. Exact scores/counts also appear in shared text because VR retains its existing three metric rails; Desktop can show all gauges. Empty cities show no average. ## Direction

Every citizen will ultimately be an authoritative data record with a stable ID, household/home membership, age/life-stage band, workplace, employment status, income, available money, consumption needs, current activity, and trip intent. Household and business ledgers will own balances and recurring transactions. The current citywide residents, employment, income, goods-demand, production, retail-capacity, sales, and spending values are deliberately defined as sums over those future records.

The simulation remains data-oriented. Citizens, households, workplaces, inventories, and trips live in packed arrays/tables updated in bounded batches. They are not one ticking Unreal Actor per person, car, or building. This preserves deterministic save/load and makes thousands of residents feasible on Quest.

## Authoritative flow

1. A resident moving into the city begins as an edge-arrival trip, reaches the construction-site/home portal, and becomes an occupant only after that site's construction completes.
2. Homes provide household capacity and desirability; citizens then seek compatible jobs reachable through the transport graph.
3. Employed citizens receive wages from commercial or industrial workplaces.
4. Households create consumption orders and can purchase only within their available budget.
5. Industry converts labor and later resource inputs into goods inventory, then dispatches freight to commercial inventories; goods that exceed local retail order capacity become explicit city-edge exports.
6. Commercial businesses open only after construction completes and delivered goods reach their inventory. They sell to household purchase trips, pay wages and costs, and generate taxable transactions.
7. Unfilled jobs, unsold inventory, unmet consumption, low household money, taxes, services, pollution, travel time, and land value feed development pressure.

Until citizen records are introduced, D32 computes these quantities as deterministic aggregate flows. New rules must keep named fields that can later be proven equal to the sum of individual records rather than inventing unrelated global demand constants.

## Movement and rendering

- Trip intent is authoritative: origin, destination, purpose, departure window, mode eligibility, route/version ID, and progress. Initial purposes are move-in arrival, commute, purchase, freight delivery, and freight export; initial modes are walk, car, and freight truck.
- Roads, sidewalks, entrances, and later transit form routing graphs. Buildings use authored entrance portals.
- Visible pedestrians and vehicles are pooled render proxies bound to nearby active trips. Off-screen trips may advance analytically between graph milestones.
- The first visible slice uses simple shared instanced pedestrian and car meshes. It must prove record-to-proxy binding and road movement before art detail, lane changing, or individual Actors are considered.
- Short reachable trips can choose walking; longer trips can choose a car or later transit based on cost and availability.
- Rendering density may be culled or represented at distance, but it must not create or delete citizens or economic transactions.
- Traffic movement has one deterministic fixed-point authority shared by rendered and off-screen vehicles. Route legs name an edge, direction, and lane; road class supplies the speed limit; same-lane leaders constrain acceleration through a safe gap; legal connectors serialize entry into outgoing lanes; lane occupancy and speed produce congestion. The board renderer consumes this state and never independently interpolates authoritative trip timing.

The shared service evaluator excludes burning/damaged providers and operational housing/job/output capacity, and gives explicit OnFire/FireDamage/Repairing diagnostics. The economy/population fixed-step loop consumes that same state, so a damaged employer removes actual job assignments and starts the existing unemployment clock; repair reopens job capacity and resets the clock when a citizen fills work. IDs, education, wallets and ledgers survive; a temporary home outage retains an existing physical assignment. No new citizen cohort or repeated wallet transfer is created by damage/load/recovery. REPAIR is an explicit treasury spend, once per repair, with a saved completion deadline. Public staffing and moving emergency vehicles remain D44; the first fire response uses road/water/power/paid-upkeep and bounded fixed capacity.

### Public employees and emergency trips (schema26)

Public employer records remain separate from business stock/reserves. Police/Fire have4 Basic-qualified slots; Power/Water have2. Civic hiring retains established employees then fills in stable citizen order before private vacancies, allowing transfers. Education/salary ceilings persist; starter salaries use the existing$5 wage. The monthly city operating debit includes public payroll once, ordinary upkeep first. Exact paid wages transfer to wallets without creating a private opening reserve, and unpaid/partial wages reduce staff output. Connected, undamaged utilities retain the authored `MinimumUtilityOutputPercent` floor (default50%); nominal200 supply therefore gives at least100 per provider during hiring/payroll shortages and200 at full paid staffing. Smaller tuned capacities retain the same percentage of their own nominal capacity. Road disconnection or fire damage still disables supply. The shared tuning rule drives authoritative snapshots and inspector output/capacity. Basic education qualifies utility workers; Secondary education is not required. Department and school capacity has no such floor. Jobs/employment and forecast/settlement reconciliation include these employers.

Emergency journeys use saved transit records (FireResponse8, PoliceResponse9, EmergencyReturn10) with station/incident associations and actual traffic vehicles (PoliceCar3, FireEngine4). Each station is busy through outward, on-scene and return phases. Completed outward trips remain on scene until the incident ends. Fire crews then choose the closest unclaimed active fire within 12 connected car-road hops of the current scene, breaking ties by incident ID; otherwise they return to their station. Scene changes retain the trip/crew identity and station association. Police crews return after the incident. Lost station operation or unpaid ordinary bills prevent onward fire assignments. No fake timer reports arrival. Road edits reset obsolete traffic paths while the intent retries; blocked journeys wait for connectivity. Prevention remains24 road hops, but crews can travel along longer routes. Actual arrival changes fire suppression and police incident duration. Public ledgers and response associations are validated with the city before load commits; schema25 cities acquire new records without backdated pay.

Schema28 permits and validates scene-origin fire responses, including live-city save/load during onward travel; older station-origin trips remain valid. Return destinations must be the associated station. Displayed water additionally waits for the interpolated engine to reach its endpoint, without delaying authoritative suppression.

### D45 minor fire recovery (2026-10-01)

An ignition still disables the building while it burns. When an arrived, operating response completes all3 suppression steps, damage at or below `FireMinorDamageAutoRepairLimit` (default30%) is removed immediately without a treasury bill. Existing derived service/capacity and pooled-mesh updates then restore the building. Above the limit, repair remains an explicit paid command; failed suppression/burnout leaves100% damage. The tuning limit is clamped0–99 (0 disables recovery), so burnout cannot qualify. Accumulated damage, rather than distance alone, accounts for road delay and unavailable response. Inspection names the recovery threshold while burning. The rule applies at incident resolution; existing resolved damage/paid repair deadlines retain their saved state. Schema29 needs no new fields because incident response steps, damage and resolution are already persisted. Pause/partial suppression save/load preserve the outcome.

## Fire pacing (D45, 2026-10-01)

Device feedback showed that9% damage per game day closed the30% recovery window in roughly7 seconds at1x and the12-day deadline burned a lot out in28.8 seconds. `FireDamagePerStep` now defaults2 (clamped1–100), with1 damage per active suppression day. Maximum burn age is50 days (120 seconds at the default2.4-second day). Three suppression days and30% free recovery remain. A9-day wait (21.6 seconds at1x) plus full suppression yields22% damage and recovers;13 unattended days plus suppression yields30% and recovers,14 yields32% and requires paid repair. Game speed scales both travel and fire; these wall-clock values are1x examples.

Spread attempts now occur every5 days of each source fire's age, rather than every3 days on the global clock. This gives newly ignited/spread buildings their full interval before spreading again. Base probability, wind/rain/protection modifiers, edge adjacency, three-active/eight-damaged caps and one new spread per step remain. Natural ignition cadence is unchanged. Incident StartStep already persists the relative schedule, so schema29 is unchanged. Existing accumulated/resolved damage is retained; new pacing applies to continuing active fires without refunds or retroactive recovery.

## Schools, education progression and skilled roles (D46, schema30)

School is an ordinary3x2 civic consumer: connected frontage, completed delivery/construction, allocated power10/water8, upkeep35 and4 Basic-qualified public staff. Critical police/fire utilities retain priority. School capacity48 scales with actual paid staff percentage, reaching zero during unavailable operation. The shared service snapshot allocates one resident education place per eligible lifetime citizen, in stable ID/provider order over24 road hops. Duplicate providers allocate only unmet places. Units are resident education places, not a child/student cohort. Homeless citizens remain in citywide demand but cannot receive the current home-based access without housing. Secondary/Higher residents no longer consume learning places.

Each citizen persists EducationMonths and LastEducationMonth. A completed billed month credits at most one learning month while that citizen has an operating accessible place and ordinary operating bills are paid. Repeated rendering, pause and repeated settlement never advance learning. Six served months move None→Basic or Basic→Secondary, resetting progress; outages retain progress. Schools stop at Secondary; Higher/college, student ages/trips and separate student cohorts remain later work. Advancement occurs after the current month's payroll/reconciliation; new qualifications affect subsequent hiring, preserving exact settled wage ledgers. Existing cities retain saved IDs/wallets/education and start the clock at the saved month without invented past learning.

Medium/high Shop/Factory definitions allocate floor(job capacity/4) Secondary-qualified skilled roles. Low-density entry jobs retain their existing accessibility. Reconciliation retains valid roles, fills remaining skilled vacancies with eligible private/unemployed workers, then fills entry vacancies; public staffing keeps its priority. Citizen bSkilledJob and business SkilledJobCapacity are saved. Skilled target wages are twice the entry wage ($10 versus$5 by default), capped by the citizen's education salary limit. Employer cash still limits payment. Wages are paid in proportion to each worker's due amount with stable-order integer remainder; credited wallets exactly equal the business debit. Education never creates an unbacked salary or private treasury subsidy. City forecasts settle a private copy through the same rules. Vacancy inspection labels requirements/target wage and actual payroll.

Education coverage feeds one bounded desirability benefit (maximum8 by default) rather than additive provider rewards. Empty-frontage coverage is derived from bounded provider reach; occupied homes use exact served/demand ratios. Graduated homes can retain the benefit while an operating reachable provider has capacity. Construction/outages/damage remove benefits and places. Education view, school capacity/staff/operation bars, home qualification/progress bars and skilled-business bars consume this same snapshot. Two appended guide milestones teach opening a school and developing qualified workers without erasing prior safety history.

Schema30 persists training progress/clock and role capacity/assignment. Older schemas default roles/progress safely and recompute the next appended guide milestone. Invalid/future clocks, out-of-range progress, unqualified skilled assignments and overfilled skilled capacity reject loads before committing a city. Normal saves retain partial learning, salaries, citizens and traffic; full-city1x/3x/5x fine/coarse serialization is tested.
