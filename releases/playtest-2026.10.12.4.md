# City Sim VR playtest - playtest-2026.10.12.4

This update publishes the refined floating Desktop interface, live building inspector and simpler tool cancellation, plus smoother water-depth colors. The Windows ZIP supports Desktop and SteamVR PCVR; the separate Quest APK includes the verified water-gradient update.

- Compact floating dark panels, a stable graphical tool dock, colorful service/zoning icons and category-colored selection/hover outlines make tools easier to identify. Prices, help and warnings stay in their own scrollable strip.
- Inspect uses a live view of the selected building's current mesh/materials instead of a baked thumbnail. Details/Upgrade and city summaries have separate panels. The preview shows the static building body; separate animated visitors/attachments and camera orbit are not included.
- Zoning stays open for switching types while painting. Esc/Cancel first aborts unfinished placement, then closes tool controls, then returns to Inspect. Idle zoning takes two presses. Idle Inspect hides Cancel, and returning to Inspect resets other dock highlights. Nested categories keep Back; redundant dock-level Desktop Back buttons are removed. Existing zoning creation and VR controls are retained.
- Shallow/deep water colors blend gradually from terrain depth, reducing square tile bands. The accepted palette, shoreline shape, water gameplay and save data are retained; shoreline edges may still look angular.

Validation includes warnings-as-errors Editor compilation, the affected shared-action regression, a focused packaged Desktop palette/Inspect flow, and extracted Windows launcher/menu/save checks. Existing compact/4K floating-layout/live-preview evidence and D86 matched water images/two focused water tests are retained. The signed Quest ARM64/Vulkan/ASTC package has min32/target35, embedded-content and signature checks. Desktop-only final polish reuses that verified Quest APK; no extra mobile rebuild, full simulation suite or performance/thermal matrix. Native inputs are synthetic; this release does not claim new physical headset rendering or comfort. The developer accepts the UI direction and water appearance overall; mode-specific observations and final-control feedback remain welcome.

This is early development for a small tester group. Future saves/assets may change incompatibly and may require a new city; this release introduces no intentional save-format break. Preserve your city files. Download the named Windows ZIP and use Play-Desktop.cmd or Play-PCVR.cmd; Quest users use the separate APK. Automatic source-code archives contain distribution documents/helpers only.
