# Current playtest limitations

- This is a Development build for feedback, not a production installer. Desktop layout/input/window/DPI and laptop coverage are still in progress.
- D3D11 is the tested Windows profile; D3D12 is not offered by the playtest launchers.
- Large cities can have CPU spikes during simulation. Desktop measurements do not establish PCVR or standalone headset performance.
- Animated shadows can still jitter. This refinement is deferred.
- Zoned growth checks every three game days; new private homes/shops/factories build three days after delivery. Zoned construction and automatic density upgrades use private funding. City-funded roads, utilities, civic facilities, repairs and purchased expansions still cost the treasury. Density demand is integrated; broad difficulty and long-term balance remain in progress.
- Custom city naming and some desktop-specific chart/slider affordances are unfinished.
- A rare utility connection issue beside ground-level avenues has been reported. If you see "not connected to road" despite an adjacent correctly facing driveway, include the affected city save and a screenshot.

- The developer has verified the listed ambience, alerts, audio recovery, utility/park art and animations, gameplay, settings and controls in Desktop and PCVR. Regular-traffic driving/idle listening, Quest standalone checks and measured dense-device audio/frame/thermal costs remain pending.

This is early development for a small tester group. City saves and assets may change incompatibly between builds; backwards compatibility is not promised. A release may require starting a new city. No save-format break is introduced by this documentation refresh. Individual release notes identify the tested modes and remaining checks.
